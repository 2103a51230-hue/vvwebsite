Our Complete Curriculum

Browse our full selection of drawing programs and specialized craft workshops. Click any class to book a free demo!

Foundational Drawing Programs

Ages 5-7

Tiny Hands & Big Colors

Focuses on basic shapes, primary colors, and fine motor skills through fun projects like color mixing.

Ages 8-10

Observational Drawing Fundamentals

Introduction to 3D form, light, shadow, and basic perspective using pencil and pen.

Ages 11-12

Master Sketchbook: Media & Scenery

Exploring advanced composition for capturing daily life objects and expressive scenery.

Ages 10+

Expressive Portraits: Charcoal & Graphite

A deep dive into tonal range, texture, and emotional depth using advanced dry media techniques.

All Ages

Lippan Art: Mud & Mirror Work

Learn the traditional Kutch art form using relief work with clay and intricate mirror embedding.

Specialty Craft Workshops

Mandala & Zentangle Art

Focus on geometric patterns, symmetry, and mindful drawing using fine liners and color pencils.

Quilling: Rolled Paper Wonders

Learn the art of rolling paper strips to create intricate 3D shapes, designs, and wall art.

Bookmarks & Gift Card Creation

Hands-on session using stamps, layered paper, and simple embossing to make personalized gifts.

Bottle Painting

Transforming recycled bottles into beautiful decorative art using various painting techniques.

Recycling Crafts

Creating amazing art from everyday waste materials. Fun, creative, and eco-friendly projects.
